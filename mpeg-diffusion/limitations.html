4.Dual-resolution attention

We fix all other factors and compare dual-resolution attention (ours) against a shared K/V cache on Wan2.1 1.3B (480p), trained for 5,000 steps. Due to the curse of mixed resolution, the shared K/V cache produces much stronger distortion along the seam.

5.Mixed-resolution decoder

We encode real videos at 480p (HR) and 240p (LR), keep the 480p latent inside a moving circle and the 240p latent outside, and compare our mixed-resolution decoder against resize-and-paste (which fills in each latent’s missing region, decodes them separately, and pastes the results together). Merging the two latents into a unified latent before decoding is critical to avoid distortion along the seam.
