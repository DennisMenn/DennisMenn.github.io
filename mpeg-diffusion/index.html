Comparison with prior and concurrent methods

Method

2.Self-selected HR region

Prior methods need a preset HR region: The early denoising steps are too blurry to externally detect the HR region from the video, so it must be given before generation.

Fix: The DiT already knows where the important subject is. A single linear layer on its intermediate features picks the HR region during denoising, so no region input is needed (examples).

4.Dual-resolution attention

Curse of mixed resolution: When HR and LR tokens share one K/V cache, we prove that no single LR token can match the attention of the four HR tokens it replaces, even with fine-tuning.

Fix: Keep separate HR and LR K/V caches so each query reads K/V at its own resolution, using a linear map to fill in the missing K/V from the other resolution (examples).

5.Mixed-resolution decoder

Resolution mismatch: Decoding the HR and LR latents separately and pasting them together causes visible distortion and a seam along the resolution boundary, even for latents encoded from real videos.

Fix: Merge the HR and LR latents into a single unified latent before decoding, so the VAE decodes a seamless video (examples).

Limitations

  • For fast-moving backgrounds, the transition between the low- and high-resolution regions can occasionally be noticeable (examples).
  • We currently train only a linear head to pick the self-selected region; end-to-end fine-tuning is a promising next step.
