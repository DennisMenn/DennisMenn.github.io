NeurIPS 2026Featured

Training-Free Latent Inter-frame Pruning with Attention Recovery

Dennis Menn, Yuedong Yang, Bokun Wang, et al.

Proposed a training-free framework that adapts pixel-space compression techniques to latent pruning for video generation. Achieved an average 1.53× throughput boost (19.3 FPS on an RTX 4090) and a 20% memory reduction, without compromising visual quality.

WACV 2026 · Under review

Synthesizing Artifact Dataset for Pixel-level Detection

Dennis Menn, Feng Liang, and Diana Marculescu

We propose a pipeline that automatically injects artifacts into clean, high-quality synthetic images on a predetermined region, thereby producing pixel-level annotations without manual labeling.

arXiv
WACV 2025 Workshop

Similarity Trajectories: Linking Sampling Process to Artifacts in Diffusion-Generated Images

Dennis Menn, Feng Liang, Hung-Yueh Chiang, and Diana Marculescu

4th Workshop on Image/Video/Audio Quality in Computer Vision and Generative AI, WACV 2025

We found that the presence of artifacts in images is related to the sampling process of the diffusion model, characterized by the "Similarity Trajectory".

Paper
Manuscript

Investigating Human-Identifiable Features Hidden In Adversarial Perturbations

Dennis Menn, Tzu-hsun Feng, Sriram Vishwanath, and Hung-yi Lee

By summing adversarial perturbations from different models, human-identifiable features emerge, showing that adversarial perturbations are in fact comprehensible to humans.

arXiv
Blog post

Can GPT Models Detect Artifacts in Images?

Dennis Menn, Alexandros Dimakis, and Diana Marculescu

GPT-4/4o models are good at recognizing objects but struggle to identify obvious errors in images generated by diffusion models. One explanation is that they only recognize key features rather than understanding the complete structure of objects.
